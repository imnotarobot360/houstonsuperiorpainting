## Rules
- One accurate profile per platform, managed by someone. Fix or remove stale ones (see `docs/external-seo-cleanup.md`) before adding new ones.
- No exact-match anchor text campaigns. Let partners link with the business name or a natural description.
- Keep a simple log of who you asked, when, and the URL once it's live. That's useful later when checking which links Google sees.
- Reviews: ask happy customers for a review on **the Google profile of the office that served them**; never offer anything in exchange, never write or post one for a customer.
