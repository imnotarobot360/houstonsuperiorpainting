# Local publication targets (categories and approach)

> INTERNAL. Categories only. No names, emails or phone numbers are listed here on purpose: find each contact on the outlet's own public website at send time and record it in the tracking sheet (see `distribution-checklist.md`). The examples below are types of outlets to research, not confirmed contacts or confirmed interest.

Focus on areas we actually serve (`SERVICE_AREAS` in `lib/business.ts`), for example Cypress, Katy, Sugar Land, Magnolia, Tomball, The Woodlands and Houston neighborhoods.

## 1. Community newspapers

- **Examples to research:** Community Impact editions covering our service areas (such as Cy-Fair, Katy, Sugar Land/Missouri City, Tomball/Magnolia, The Woodlands), plus other local community papers.
- **Approach:** use the outlet's news-tip or "submit news" form. Lead with the homeowner angle (Houston humidity and painting prep), not the company. Some run short business-news items about local companies; ask which section fits.
- **Hook:** seasonal timing for exterior painting; what to ask a contractor before hiring.

## 2. Houston Chronicle home and real estate

- **Approach:** find the home, real estate or lifestyle writers on the Chronicle's own staff or news-tip pages. Offer Juan as a source for future stories on home maintenance or preparing a house to sell, rather than asking for a story about the guide.
- **Hook:** what to ask before hiring a painter; why paint fails early in humid weather. Expect long lead times; being quoted as a source is more realistic than a feature.

## 3. Neighborhood and HOA newsletters

- **Approach:** contact the HOA or community association management company through its public contact page. Offer the guide as a free homeowner resource, or a short educational article on preparing a home for exterior painting (including tips on HOA color approval). Make clear it is not an ad.
- **Notes:** some newsletters only accept paid ads; do not pay for placement under this plan. Never use residents' personal contact details from a directory.

## 4. Chambers of commerce

- **Examples to research:** chambers in our service areas (such as Cy-Fair, Katy Area, Fort Bend, Tomball, Magnolia, The Woodlands area). [VERIFY: which chambers, if any, Houston Superior Painting belongs to]
- **Approach:** member-news submissions and newsletters are usually members-only. If we are not a member, skip it or ask Juan whether joining is worthwhile. Never imply membership we don't have.

## 5. Supplier and manufacturer newsletters

- **Examples to research:** Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore and Farrow & Ball (the brands we use, per `BUSINESS.paintPartners`) and the local paint stores we buy from.
- **Approach:** ask our store rep whether they run a contractor spotlight, blog or local newsletter. Never describe us as a "preferred contractor/partner" of any brand, and don't use brand logos without permission.

## 6. Home-improvement podcasts and blogs

- **Approach:** look for Houston and Texas home, real estate and DIY podcasts and blogs. Pitch Juan as a guest with two or three concrete topics (painting prep in a humid climate, how to read a painting estimate, HOA color approvals). Local shows first.
- **Avoid:** paid guest posts and "featured on" placements.

## 7. Local TV home segments

- **Approach:** morning and weekend lifestyle or home segments on Houston stations, through each station's public news-tip or segment-request page. Pitch a short, visual demonstration, such as three prep steps that keep exterior paint from peeling.
- **Notes:** confirm Juan is willing to appear on camera before pitching (he does not use an owner photo on the website); if not, offer a crew lead or skip this category. Some lifestyle segments are sponsored; do not pay under this plan.

## Out of scope

- Paid newswires and press-release distribution services
- Sponsored articles, paid "as seen on" placements, and award or "best of" programs that charge a fee
- Review sites and directories (handled separately)
