# Distribution checklist

> INTERNAL. Everything in `docs/press/` is a draft. Nothing is sent until section 1 is fully checked.

## 1. Approvals (all required before anything is sent)

- [ ] Guide page is live at https://houstonsuperiorpainting.com/houston-painting-contractor-guide (returns 200, is not a redirect, is in the sitemap)
- [ ] Juan confirms each of the 10 points in the 10-Point Preparation Standard matches what crews actually do, and the release wording matches the guide page
- [ ] Juan picks and approves the quote in writing (`owner-quote-for-approval.md`)
- [ ] Juan approves the boilerplate (`boilerplate.md`) and the 5-year workmanship warranty wording
- [ ] Media contact name, phone and email filled in (who answers reporter calls, and how quickly)
- [ ] Every placeholder resolved or deleted: search the files for `[VERIFY` and `[QUOTE` before sending
- [ ] Final claims check: no "licensed", "bonded", BBB, office count, ratings, review counts, project counts, "best/#1/leading"
- [ ] Juan approves the final release text and the list of outlets

## 2. Channel rules

- No paid newswire (PR Newswire, Business Wire, EIN Presswire and similar). The release would be syndicated to sites we don't control for little value.
- One-to-one, personalized emails only. No mass BCC, no purchased media lists.
- Take contacts from each outlet's own public "contact us", "submit news" or staff page. Do not guess email addresses.
- Paste the pitch in the body and the release below it (or as a link). No large attachments.
- If an outlet has a submission form, use the form instead of email.
- Send Tuesday to Thursday mornings. One follow-up after about a week, then stop.

## 3. Who to send to, in order

Categories and how to approach each are in `local-publication-targets.md`.

1. Community newspapers covering our service areas (news-tip or community submission forms)
2. Chambers of commerce we belong to [VERIFY: which, if any] (member news)
3. Neighborhood and HOA newsletters (offer the guide as a homeowner resource, not an ad)
4. Supplier and manufacturer newsletters (ask our store reps about contractor spotlights)
5. Houston Chronicle home and real estate coverage (offer Juan as a source)
6. Home-improvement podcasts and blogs (pitch Juan as a guest)
7. Local TV home and lifestyle segments (pitch a short, visual prep demonstration)

## 4. Tracking

Keep a sheet outside this repo (it will hold people's contact details) with these columns:

| Outlet | Category | Contact (from outlet's public page) | Date sent | Channel (email/form) | Follow-up date | Response | Outcome (link / mention / interview / none) | Live URL | Links to guide? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- Log every send the same day.
- When coverage runs, save the URL, note whether it links to the guide, and thank the writer.
- Never ask a writer to change anchor text to a keyword.
- Review results 30 days after the first send.

## 5. After coverage

- [ ] Keep coverage links in an internal list for Juan; do not add "as seen in" badges to the site without his approval
- [ ] If an outlet asks for photos, use only photos Juan has confirmed as our work (`lib/projects.ts`, entries without `photosNeedReview`)
- [ ] If a reporter wants a customer to interview, ask Juan first, and never share a customer's details without that customer's permission
